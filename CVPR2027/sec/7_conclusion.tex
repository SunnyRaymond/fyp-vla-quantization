\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, a latent-aware quantization framework for 4-bit world action models.
Once the action pathway of a 4-bit WAM is protected, its remaining action error enters through a degraded predicted future, which is damaged most by errors that are written once and read at every denoising step.
\method preserves the future where it is written, stored and read: it measures future error as the reader sees it, weights each rounding error by how many times it is read, allocates precision jointly over weights, activations and caches, and gives the tokens the reader attends to a second 4-bit pass.
With every weight at 4 bits and no recalibration per task, it recovers closed-loop success across two WAMs in simulation and on a real robot arm.
When compressing a WAM, the predicted future is not an intermediate quantity that can be sacrificed.
