\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method, a latent-aware vector quantization framework for world action models.
Under ultra-low-bit weight quantization, a WAM loses the fidelity of its predicted future, and most of its action error follows from this loss.
\method makes the predicted future an explicit quantization target: it measures future error as the reader sees it, spends bits where they preserve the future, initializes the codes with trellis-coded rounding at mixed bit widths, and calibrates the shared decode tables toward action and future fidelity.
At the same bits per weight as existing vector quantizers and with no extra storage, it recovers closed-loop success across \XX WAMs in simulation and on a real robot arm.
When compressing a WAM, the predicted future is not an intermediate quantity that can be sacrificed.
