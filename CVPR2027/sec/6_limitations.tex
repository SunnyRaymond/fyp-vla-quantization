\section{Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations}

Future work can extend \method in several directions.
First, we quantize weights only and keep activations in BF16; combining latent-aware weight quantization with low-bit activations would further reduce memory traffic and latency.
Second, our evaluation covers \XX WAMs, and additional architectures and scales would further test how broadly future-latent fidelity can guide quantization.
Third, our real-world experiments cover three manipulation tasks on a single arm, and our efficiency measurements use \XX.
Extending them to more tasks, robot platforms and embedded onboard computers would give a broader picture of the practical benefits of \method.
