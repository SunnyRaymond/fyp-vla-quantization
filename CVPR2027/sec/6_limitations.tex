\section{Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our evaluation covers two WAMs; architectures that reuse their caches across control steps would further test how broadly the read count can guide quantization.
The gaze is carried from one control step to the next, so scenes that change abruptly may call for faster tracking.
Our real-world experiments cover three tasks on a single arm and our efficiency measurements a single data-center GPU. Expanding to more tasks, robot platforms and embedded onboard computers would broaden LAQ's impact.
LAQ also points to two primitives that current accelerators lack: matrix units that give a few selected tokens a second low-bit pass natively, and fused attention units that also emit the attention mass each key receives. Hardware that supports them would make reader-aware precision a first-class operation.
