\section{Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations}

Future work can extend \method in several directions.
First, our evaluation covers two WAMs; architectures that reuse their caches across control steps would further test how broadly the read count can guide quantization.
Second, the gaze is carried from one control step to the next, and scenes that change abruptly between control steps may call for faster tracking.
Third, our real-world experiments cover three manipulation tasks on a single arm, and our efficiency measurements use a single data-center GPU, on which the 4-bit runtime saves memory but not time.
Extending them to more tasks, robot platforms and embedded onboard computers would give a broader picture of the practical benefits of \method.
