# RankCal: Counterfactual Influence Quantization for Action-Conditioned Latent World-Model Planning

**Method.** RankCal (Counterfactual Influence Quantization)

## Motivation
**Problem framing.** Phase 1 identifies a structural bottleneck: PTQ effects in world-model planning vary with bit-width, granularity, module, and horizon, so the planning objective can remain apparently healthy while task success falls. In this setting, the planner scores imagined action sequences through an action-conditioned latent dynamics model; a local perturbation matters because it can reorder candidates, alter the selected action, and change later latent states under receding-horizon closed-loop execution. A tensor-MSE heuristic measures the wrong object when the downstream decision is an ordering, not a reconstruction target.

`arxiv:2602.02110v1` provides the closest empirical anchor with systematic weight-only and joint W/A sweeps, but the proposed branch asks for a site-level counterfactual estimand that holds planner inputs and all other sites fixed. `arxiv:2608.24855v1` changes the planning procedure through latent trajectory generation and rollout reranking, and `arxiv:2603.12231v3` changes latent geometry through temporal straightening; neither turns quantization sensitivity into an allocator for the frozen planner. `arxiv:2607.28405v1` is a related but separate WAM PTQ direction centered on iterative joint video/action denoising, not imagined action-sequence latent-dynamics planning.

**Why now.** Recent work makes the gap operationally testable without changing the planner: the full-text-backed `arxiv:2608.24855v1` studies latent trajectory generation and rollout reranking, the connector-verified but abstract-only `arxiv:2607.28405v1` exposes deployment-mismatched WAM PTQ concerns, and the full-text-backed `arxiv:2602.02110v1` supplies the closest systematic world-model quantization sweep. Public visual continuous-control tooling and local inference make a site-only counterfactual probe practical, while explicit horizon-wise planning and backend-level byte accounting turn the question into a bounded decision-level audit; actual mixed W/A backend support and measured `PeakBytes` remain implementation conditions rather than assumptions.

**Why prior work stopped.**
- `arxiv:2602.02110v1` (arXiv 2026): `arxiv:2602.02110v1` systematically swept weight-only and joint W/A PTQ across methods, granularity, modules, and planning horizons, and reported mismatch between planning objectives and task success.
  - _Did not do_: It did not define a frozen-model, site-only counterfactual candidate-order influence estimand and use that profile to solve a measured `PeakBytes`-constrained mixed W/A allocation.
  - _Structural reason_: Its measurement axes and aggregate outcomes did not include a one-site intervention that connects candidate-order discordance in imagined rollouts to the bit allocator.
- `arxiv:2608.24855v1` (arXiv 2026-08): `arxiv:2608.24855v1` replaced repeated CEM search with rectified-flow latent trajectory generation, inverse-dynamics decoding, and frozen-LeWM rollout reranking.
  - _Did not do_: It did not alter PTQ calibration or bit allocation for a frozen latent planner using site-specific ranking influence from multi-step quantized rollouts.
  - _Structural reason_: Its contribution redesigns how plans are generated rather than measuring and allocating numerical precision according to decision-level sensitivity.
- `arxiv:2603.12231v3` (arXiv 2026-03): `arxiv:2603.12231v3` used a curvature regularizer to straighten local latent trajectories and improve the conditioning of latent distance and planning objectives.
  - _Did not do_: It did not estimate site-only counterfactual ranking influence under quantized action-conditioned rollouts or use that estimate for mixed-precision PTQ.
  - _Structural reason_: It changes representation geometry during training, whereas RankCal needs a post-training intervention and allocation mechanism for a fixed planner and latent dynamics model.
- `arxiv:2607.28405v1` (arXiv 2026): The connector-verified but abstract-only `arxiv:2607.28405v1` proposed coordinate-compatible activation evidence, joint video-action saliency, and fixed-intervention auditing for World Action Model PTQ.
  - _Did not do_: It did not target imagined action-sequence latent-dynamics planner rankings or derive weight/activation bits from a site-only rollout-ranking influence profile.
  - _Structural reason_: Its branch follows iterative joint video/action denoising and WAM coupling, so its intervention target is different from closed-loop consequences of imagined action-sequence planning.

**What changes when the gap closes.** Closing the gap would make mixed-precision PTQ a decision-preservation tool for imagined action-sequence planners, exposing when two allocations with similar nominal bit-width have different candidate-order and closed-loop consequences. It would give the broad sweep in `arxiv:2602.02110v1` a site-level diagnostic interpretation and a deployable allocator interface, while remaining orthogonal to the planner redesign in `arxiv:2608.24855v1` and separate from `arxiv:2607.28405v1`'s WAM joint video/action denoising branch.

## Method
**Pipeline.** RankCal takes a frozen FP16 action-conditioned latent world model, planner inputs, candidate action sequences, and quantization site/bit-pair options. It holds the planner and rollout convention fixed, quantizes one site at a time, and measures how each intervention changes the ordering of imagined action sequences over latent dynamics. The resulting site-by-bit influence profile is converted into a mixed W/A assignment by minimizing influence under measured `PeakBytes` and a fixed nominal bit histogram. The output is a quantized checkpoint for the unchanged receding-horizon planner, whose candidate ordering and closed-loop action consequences remain the objects of evaluation; joint video/action denoising in WAMs is outside this branch.

### M1_background
*Set up the frozen imagined-rollout probe that the contribution is built on.*

1. **Freeze the imagined-rollout probe** (`S1`)
   - Start from the frozen FP16 action-conditioned latent world model and create a `site_manifest` with one stable site ID for every quantizable weight or activation site, its module or operator path, tensor shape and layout, calibration-tensor source, allowed bit pairs, and execution status. Freeze the planner inputs, candidate action-sequence pool with stable candidate IDs, latent initial states, rollout horizons, receding-horizon schedule, scalar score returned by the existing planner objective, and tie rule. The evaluator must emit that score and the latent-state trace for each candidate and horizon. Select and record a concrete target inference backend and support manifest: a pair is `backend_native` only when the deployed kernel and storage layout execute it; `torch.ao` fake-quant/dequant is `emulation_only` when it only simulates the arithmetic. 【author decision: name the target backend and the supported W/A kernel/layout matrix, and decide which unsupported pairs are emulation-only】 For each site and allowed bit pair, quantize only that site, keep every other site in FP16, and replay the identical inputs, order, declared seeds, calibration procedure, and tie rule. Record quantizer parameters, backend and runtime provenance, and execution mode. Return aligned FP16 and one-site outputs keyed by site ID, bit pair, horizon, and candidate ID; never merge native and emulated rows.

*Roll out each candidate action sequence through the frozen action-conditioned latent dynamics.*
$$ z_{t+1}=F_{\theta}(z_t,a_t),\quad \tau(a_{0:H-1})=(z_0,a_0,z_1,\ldots,a_{H-1},z_H) \tag{1} $$

   - _Why:_ This isolates a site-level perturbation in the action-conditioned latent-dynamics branch and prevents planner redesign or WAM denoising changes from being mistaken for quantization effects.

### M2_rankcal
*Measure site-by-bit rollout-ranking influence and convert it into the memory-constrained mixed W/A assignment.*

2. **Estimate rollout-ranking influence** (`S2`)
   - For each site ID and allowed bit pair, replay the frozen probe on exactly the same candidate pool, latent initial states, declared horizons, and seeds. Define `P` as every unordered pair of distinct candidate IDs in the fixed pool, and use the planner's existing scalar score for both the FP16 vector and the one-site vector. Apply the pairwise agreement definition in E2 to their score differences; retain the resulting horizon-specific $I_{g}(b)$ as the site's ranking influence. A zero score difference remains a tie for this comparison, and the the tie rule fixed in the frozen-probe step is used only when the planner must select an action; do not add jitter or a new score. If rollout execution is stochastic, retain per-seed scores and apply one fixed aggregation rule to every row. 【author decision: choose single-trace or seed-set aggregation and fix whether aggregation occurs before or after pairwise comparison】 Store one row per site, bit pair, horizon, and execution mode with the agreement, $I_{g}(b)$, score provenance, calibration revision, and backend status. Keep native and `torch.ao` emulation rows separate; only rows admitted by the the bit-allocation support manifest may feed the deployable assignment.

*Measure pairwise candidate-order agreement after quantizing only site g with bit pair b, then convert disagreement into influence.*
$$ A_g(b)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{P}|}\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal{P}}\mathbf{1}\left[\operatorname{sgn}(s_i-s_j)=\operatorname{sgn}(s_i^{(g,b)}-s_j^{(g,b)})\right],\quad I_g(b)=1-A_g(b) \tag{2} $$

   - _Why:_ The ranking estimand closes the gap between tensor reconstruction error and the planner's decision-level sensitivity, including the downstream consequence of an altered selected action.
3. **Allocate bits under measured memory** (`S3`)
   - Take the profile from S2, the finite bit-pair menu, the backend support manifest, the target bit histogram `h`, and the stated `PeakBytes` ceiling B. Let each complete assignment give one weight/activation bit pair to every quantizable site. Define the fixed histogram constraint before searching and record its counting unit and pair-count vector. 【author decision: choose site-count or element-weighted histogram semantics and fix exact byte equality or a predeclared matching tolerance before seeing results】 For every candidate assignment, materialize the complete checkpoint in the target backend and measure end-to-end `PeakBytes` over the same full planner trace: from the declared checkpoint-load boundary through the final latent rollout, include model storage, scales and zero-points, backend workspaces, planner state, candidate storage, latent states, and temporary buffers, and use the same allocator and runtime instrumentation for every assignment. 【author decision: fix the end-to-end measurement boundary and allocator counter, including whether the reported value is device allocated or reserved bytes】 Do not use nominal bit counts or bytes observed from a `torch.ao` emulation as native `PeakBytes`; record emulation measurements separately. Enumerate complete assignments, keep only those satisfying the fixed histogram and measured `PeakBytes` ceiling, and select the assignment with the smallest aggregate $I_{g}(b)$ under E3; the output manifest must list every site's pair, support status, measured peak, and histogram. Construct the uniform-bit and local-reconstruction comparators through the same site manifest, calibration data, backend, histogram, and `PeakBytes` harness before comparing them. 【author decision: define the local reconstruction scalar and its weight/activation normalization, and define how a uniform comparator is represented when exact histogram or measured-PeakBytes matching is unavailable】 For any reported matched comparison, require identical histogram assignments and the same measured `PeakBytes` matching rule across RankCal, both comparators, and the permutation control; otherwise mark the stratum unmatched.

*Choose the mixed W/A assignment with the smallest aggregate ranking influence under a measured peak-memory budget and fixed bit histogram.*
$$ \mathbf{b}^{\star}=\arg\min_{\mathbf{b}}\sum_{g\in\mathcal{G}}I_g(b_g)\quad\text{s.t.}\quad\operatorname{PeakBytes}(\mathbf{b})\le B,\;\operatorname{Hist}(\mathbf{b})=\mathbf{h} \tag{3} $$

   - _Why:_ This converts the diagnostic profile into a deployable mixed W/A PTQ assignment without changing the latent planner or replacing measured memory with a nominal byte assumption.

### M3_validation
*Evaluate candidate ordering, first-action agreement, and closed-loop task success for the unchanged planner.*

4. **Preserve closed-loop action consequences** (`S4`)
   - Materialize `b*` across the complete quantizable-site set and run the actual target-backend checkpoint with the unchanged planner. Use one versioned `evaluation_manifest` containing the task and episode IDs, initial states, candidate pool, declared horizons, replanning interval, stopping rule, random seeds, and existing success predicate; replay it identically for FP16, RankCal, the uniform-bit and local-reconstruction comparators, and the permutation control. 【author decision: name the evaluation manifest and all of its task, seed, horizon, and success fields before any comparison】 At every planning point, save the full candidate order, E2-based candidate-order agreement against FP16, the first selected action after the the tie rule fixed in the frozen-probe step, and the existing closed-loop task-success result; aggregate by horizon and episode without changing the planner. Record `Hist(b)`, measured end-to-end `PeakBytes`, native or emulation status, and the byte-matching stratum with each result, and evaluate the final checkpoint with all sites assigned rather than reusing only one-site probes. Inside this same all-sites evaluation, select the predeclared representative high and low site pairs and construct complete matched variants: force the high site only, the low site only, or both jointly, while completing all other sites with the same declared assignment rule. Measure each variant with the identical candidate pool, histogram, measured `PeakBytes` target, planner schedule, receding-horizon convention, and tie rule; use the same E2-derived candidate-order discordance for the single-site and joint rows. 【author decision: fix the pair-selection rule, horizon and bit stratum, tie-break, and full-assignment completion before observing outcomes】 If an exact histogram-and-byte match cannot be constructed, set the pair diagnostic status to `unavailable` and do not compare the rows. Treat the diagnostic as tactical evidence inside the all-sites evaluation only: do not define a new estimand or summary target, add a step ID, claim joint optimality, or change the pre-existing permutation-based falsification direction; any discrepancy may only bound the interpretation of the site-wise allocation signal.
   - _Why:_ The object to preserve is the selected action and its later consequences, so the evaluation stays in latent-dynamics planning rather than switching to joint video/action denoising. The pair diagnostic tests the interaction boundary inside the same all-sites evaluation without changing the site-only estimand or falsification prediction.
